## How to Ruin Your Day Before Breakfast 🫠
Have you ever noticed that sometimes the day has barely started and you already feel mentally scattered?

not tired,

not lazy,

just unfocused. Like your brain woke up, but never fully landed.

I know that feeling very well...

### I struggle with focus :)

I often do several things at once.

Not because I want to, but because something comes to mind, and I feel like I need to do it immediately BEFORE I FORGET

So I switch,

then I switch again.

And somewhere in the middle, the first thing stays unfinished...

Then another task appears, and another, and at the end, 

I feel busy,

but not clear.

Active,

but not effective.

And honestly, it is frustrating .-.

Because it feels like I am always doing *SOMETHING*

but not always getting *ANYWHERE*.

### I started noticing it most in the morning

For me,

morning is the most sensitive part of the day.

After sleep my mind feels quieter,

cleaner,

like it has had a chance to rest and reset.

And then sometimes,

almost immediately I interrupt that..

I reach for my phone

- I check messages
- notifications
- instagram, etc.
  
Whatever is there.

And before I am even fully awake, my attention is already gone :(

but not with me.

- with other people
- other information
- other input ...

### And I started thinking: Maybe this is where the problem begins

It is not really about the phone, the phone is not the enemy.

The real issue, 

for me,

is what happens to my mind when the first thing I give it is fast, fragmented input.

Because then my brain starts working the same way ->

Fast.

Fragmented.

Jumping.

-> and that feeling follows me

I forget things more easily.

I lose structure.

I start one task, then another, then another.

And later I wonder why everything feels harder than it should?

### So I started trying small changes

Nothing dramatic :)

Just small things that help me wake up into my own mind *FIRST*.

Sometimes I start with basic routine.

- Just getting up,
- getting ready,
- letting myself wake up slowly.

Sometimes I write.

Not just dreams. Anything..

Because journaling helps me organize my thoughts.

It gives them structure and turns mental noise into something clearer.

Sometimes I read a few pages of a book.

And that helps for a different reason.

A book asks me to stay with one thought,

to follow one line.

To focus without jumping.

And when I really do not have time, but still want to avoid that instant flood of input,

I open Sudoku and play a few games.

and weirdly enough, that helps too :D

Because it gives my brain logic and order instead of more chaos.

### I do not do this perfectly

Sometimes I still scroll.

Sometimes I ignore my own advice.

But I have noticed the *DIFFERENCE*

When I start the morning in a calmer, more structured way,

*MY WHOLE DAY FEELS EASIER*!

I feel less scattered,

I think more clearly.

I move through things with more order in both my thoughts and my actions.

### And I think I am not the only one

A lot of people struggle with focus now.

With finishing things, with structure, with feeling present in what they are doing.

And maybe that struggle begins in the *FIRST FEW MINUTES* after we wake up.

### So my point is

Maybe one way to ruin your day before breakfast is to give your brain too much, too fast,

before it has even had time to become your own.

And maybe one way to make the day easier

is to protect that first moment a little more <3
