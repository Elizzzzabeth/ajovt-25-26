###### * welcome to the process of thinking about "Today I Learned" talk, and how it was formed (my raw notes)

## The main idea for a talk

When I was thinking about what my talk should be about, I remembered a reflection I once wrote down for myself about a year ago. I often have lots of thoughts that keep me from falling asleep, and this was one of them. The next morning, I tried to write it down and give it some structure so that I could later share it with someone.

###### -> translated from cz:

*"Our brain is like a computer ~ it needs updates and a restart so it can rest, remove unnecessary information, and free up memory. Our brain does the same thing during sleep.*

*1. PRIORITIZATION*
   
*Within a given time frame, we perform our own restart. We set aside anxious thoughts, and our psyche, just like our body ~ our physical self ~ has a chance to rest and regenerate.*
*When we wake up, our software is emptied out and “clean.” It needs to be started up again.*

*a) Commonly:*

*We pick up our phone~notifications, messages, fast content.*

*Our memory quickly fills with a huge amount of information. The flow of information is intense and unstructured. It boosts dopamine and our appetite for the new day, but then, like the effects of caffeine, sugar, or nicotine, it leads to a harsh crash. Ordinary everyday tasks become difficult. Our motivation for activities and tasks becomes significantly lower. We constantly need more stimulation, because slowly but surely we are falling into a dopamine pit.*

*Our thinking and attention become scattered. We forget things, overthink, feel anxious, and fall into stress during the day as well as during sleep.*

*Our software needs a restart.*

*The following days unfold in a similar way.*

*Our computer cannot keep up with updating, our brain cannot keep up with resting, our psyche cannot keep up with regenerating, and neither can our hardware ~ our physical body.*

*The PC is KO.*

*b) A possible solution:*

*1. Get up and start the day with routine activities.
RISK ~ falling back asleep.
BENEFIT ~ a slow start for the body and mind, from a clean slate.*

*2. Write down your dreams.
PROBLEM ~ the software is empty, updated.
BENEFIT ~ our brain begins functioning by training memory.*

*3. Read a book.
PROBLEM ~ time, concentration.
BENEFIT ~ books are written by authors who present their stories or thoughts in a structured way, so our brain trains logical, organized thinking."*

### What follows from this?

Looking back, I decided to return to this reflection and expand on it through my own experience, because the problem I am talking about has not gone away. In fact, the more I reflect on it and speak about it, the more I try to do something about it and change the way I normally function into something healthier and more beneficial for both me and my life.

At its core, this is about my struggle with concentration. I often find myself doing several things at once ~ not because I want to, but because a new thought comes to mind and, afraid I might forget it, I start dealing with it immediately before I have finished the previous task. Maybe it is a little bit ADHD ~ who knows ~ but to me, it feels like a problem that many people are dealing with today because of social media. We become distracted, and then frustrated. I often find myself in situations where I leave things unfinished or feel as though I never have enough time and never manage to get everything done.

That is why I decided to start working on it myself. Not having TikTok is not the only solution when Instagram and Reels still exist. Lately, I have been genuinely trying to address this, because fast videos and the constant overload of information from all directions clearly affect the way we think. Even though I still have moments when I get stuck on social media, I try to minimize it especially in the morning.

Based on this, I decided that my talk would move in this direction. So, if this topic interests you, you can watch it here: [Today I Learned ~ talk 🧐](/ajovt-25-26/today-i-learned/index)
